Privacy Policy

Dusk Wallpapers Pro · Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dusk Wallpapers Pro ("the App") handles information when you use it. The App does not require you to create an account or sign in.

1. Information and app usage

We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, or other account details to use the App. We do not operate our own advertising or cross-app advertising tracking in the App.

When you browse or download wallpapers, technical information may be processed by the services used to deliver the App and its content. Depending on how those services are configured, this may include information such as your device type, operating system version, IP address, and request or diagnostic information. We do not use this information to identify you personally.

2. In-app purchases and RevenueCat

The App offers an optional in-app purchase that unlocks premium wallpapers. Purchases are made through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

We use RevenueCat to help manage purchases, verify purchase status, and provide purchase-related analytics. RevenueCat may process information such as an app-specific or anonymous user identifier, product and transaction information, purchase history, subscription or entitlement status (where applicable), and technical information about the device and app. The App's premium unlock is a non-consumable purchase; it is not a subscription.

Apple and RevenueCat process information under their own privacy policies. You can learn more at Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

3. Analytics and diagnostics

We have not intentionally connected a separate third-party analytics service such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or PostHog. RevenueCat's purchase-related analytics are used as described above.

The tools or infrastructure used to build, operate, and deliver the App may process limited technical, diagnostic, or operational information. The exact information depends on the services enabled in the published app and their configuration. We will update this Policy if we enable additional analytics or data-collection services.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by Apple, RevenueCat, and service providers that support app operation, content delivery, hosting, or diagnostics, to the extent needed to provide those services. Those providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Data retention and deletion

We do not maintain user accounts or a user profile that you can sign in to. Purchase records and related identifiers may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to process transactions, prevent fraud, provide purchase restoration, comply with legal obligations, and operate their services. For requests concerning information held by those providers, please consult their privacy policies and contact them directly.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the details below.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can contact us with privacy questions or requests. Some purchase and transaction information is controlled by Apple or RevenueCat, and requests about that information may need to be made to the relevant provider.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact: privacy@duskwallpapers.com
