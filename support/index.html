Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock premium wallpapers?

Open the premium section in the app and follow the purchase instructions. Purchases are completed securely through Apple's in-app purchase system.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you've previously purchased the premium unlock, use the Restore Purchases option in the app, if available. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. If the purchase still doesn't restore, contact support.

Who handles payments and refunds?

Payments are processed by Apple. For billing questions or refund requests, visit Apple's Report a Problem page. We can't access or change your payment card details.

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. Dusk Wallpapers Pro does not require you to create an account or sign in to use the app.

How do I report a problem with a wallpaper?

Email our support team with the name or a description of the wallpaper and explain what seems to be wrong.
